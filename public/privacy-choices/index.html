Website privacy

Privacy and measurement choices

Updated November 4, 2026. This page supplements our Privacy Policy with details about website measurement. You can request a quote whether optional OpenAI measurement is on or off.

Your choice in this browser

Use the controls below to choose whether optional OpenAI measurement is allowed.

This choice applies to this browser. We also honor Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track for this integration. A browser privacy signal keeps optional OpenAI measurement off even if you select Allow.

What we measure

We use the OpenAI Pixel to understand whether ChatGPT ads lead to website visits, phone-button clicks and successfully submitted quote requests. A phone-button click does not tell us whether a call connected. A quote request is measured only after our website confirms receipt, and does not mean a job was booked.

The Pixel may send page information, event timing, browser and device information, and ad click identifiers to OpenAI. It uses first-party cookies, including __oppref and __obref, for measurement. If automatic advanced matching is enabled for our data source, supported contact information entered into forms may be normalized and hashed in your browser before it is sent. Our event code does not include your name, property address, message or raw email or phone number.

Turning optional OpenAI measurement off prevents this integration from loading or sending future events, removes its available measurement cookies and clears its locally stored source details. This control does not change other website services or tracking described in the existing Privacy Policy.

How an inquiry's source is recorded

When optional measurement is allowed, we retain available campaign tags, ad click references, the entry page and the referring website in this browser for up to 30 days. We add a readable source summary to your quote request so our team can see how you found us. We do not copy the referring page's private path or query into that summary. Direct visits, privacy settings and blocked tracking can leave the source unavailable.

The source summary follows the same quote workflow as the information you submit. Your existing message is preserved. If adding the summary would exceed the form's message limit, we leave your message unchanged.
